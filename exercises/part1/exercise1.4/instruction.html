What to Do When the Power Comes Back On

These instructions are for household members to follow when electricity returns after a power cut. Follow them carefully to help prevent electrical accidents.

What You Need

Prepare these items to help you stay safe when electricity returns.

  • A working flashlight
  • Contact details for a qualified electrician
  • Safety information for your electrical appliances

Steps

Follow these steps carefully when electricity returns.

  1. Stay away from exposed wires. Never touch damaged cables or electrical equipment.
  2. Check from a safe distance whether the power supply appears normal.
  3. Keep sensitive appliances switched off until the supply is stable.
  4. Turn on appliances one at a time, following the manufacturer's instructions.
  5. If you notice sparks, smoke, or a burning smell, move away and seek professional help.

If Something Goes Wrong

If you notice sparks, smoke, or damaged wiring, do not touch the equipment or attempt to repair it yourself. Move to a safe place and contact a qualified electrician or the appropriate emergency service.

When electricity returns, be especially careful around damaged equipment. Do not take risks just to restore power quickly.

Keep the contact details of a QE available for emergencies.
