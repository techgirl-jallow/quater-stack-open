How to Apply for a National ID Card

Applying for a national ID card requires preparing the necessary documents and submitting an application at the immigration office.

Before You Go

Before going to the immigration office, make sure you have your parents' ID cards and your birth certificate.

What to Bring

Prepare the required documents before leaving home.

Parents' ID Cards

Bring your parents' ID cards as required for your application.

Birth Certificate

Bring your birth certificate to provide proof of your birth and personal details.

At the Office

Go to the immigration office and submit your application along with the required documents.

After Your Application

Ask the office staff about the next steps, when your ID card will be ready, and how to collect it.
